What CropGuard Offers

🌾

Smart Crop Planner

Uses real-time weather data and AI logic to recommend the best crops for your region. Helps farmers avoid climate risks, choose profitable crops, and plan their entire farming season intelligently.

📊

Real-Time Market Price Tracker

Fetches fresh mandi prices using a secure API, stores them in your database, and helps farmers decide the best market and best time to sell their crops based on live trends.

🚜

Community Equipment Sharing

Allows farmers to share, rent, and lend equipment within their community — reducing costs and avoiding unnecessary purchases. Tracks rented and returned dates automatically.

📘

Farmer Learning Hub

A growing library of tutorials, guides, seasonal crop tips, and modern farming techniques that empower farmers with the right knowledge at the right time.

🤖

AI Chatbot

Your personal assistant for farming queries — available 24/7. Ask about diseases, fertilizer use, soil tips, weather insights, and more.

📓

Smart Farm Diary

Helps farmers record daily farm activities, expenses, rainfall, treatments, and track growth — all neatly organized and searchable anytime.
